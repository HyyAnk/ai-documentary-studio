# Research Rules

- Separate verified facts from inference and open questions.
- Keep sources close to the claim they support.
- Never create research artifacts for an unconfirmed topic candidate.
- Assign every usable claim a stable ID (`C01`, `C02`, ...).
- Cite a direct primary or authoritative URL for every material claim; a search-results URL is not a source.
- Build a chronology before writing conclusions. Capture dates, named programs, institutions, decisions, measurable results, and contemporary expectations.
- Record conflicting evidence and unresolved questions instead of smoothing them over.
- Include a visual evidence inventory: archives, documents, maps, diagrams, locations, objects, and reconstruction opportunities tied to claim IDs.
